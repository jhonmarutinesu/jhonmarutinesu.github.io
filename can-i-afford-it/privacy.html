Privacy Policy for Can I Afford It?

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Can I Afford It? ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Overview

Can I Afford It? is a financial forecasting and simulation app designed to help users understand the impact of purchases on their future finances.

The App is designed to work fully offline and does not require any account or login.

2. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal data.

The App does not:

  • Require account registration
  • Collect email addresses or names
  • Access bank or financial accounts
  • Collect location data
  • Use analytics or tracking tools
  • Send data to external servers

3. Local Storage

All data entered into the App is stored locally on your device only. This may include:

  • Income and expense settings
  • Balance and buffer configurations
  • Expense templates and schedules
  • Forecast simulation data
  • Savings goals
  • App preferences (such as currency settings)

This data never leaves your device.

4. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally, security depends on your device’s built-in protections. We do not have access to your data at any time.

Uninstalling the App or clearing app data will permanently delete all stored information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

If future versions introduce ads or external services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
