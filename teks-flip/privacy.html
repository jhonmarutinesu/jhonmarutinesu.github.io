Privacy Policy for Teks Flip

Effective date: April 6, 2026

Teks Flip ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and your choices when using the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Your name and email address when you sign in using Google (Gmail login)

Information collected automatically (by third-party services):

  • Device information such as device type and operating system
  • App usage data and gameplay activity
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash and diagnostic information

2. Google Sign-In

Teks Flip allows users to sign in using their Google account. When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive basic profile information such as your name and email address.

This information is used only for:

  • Account identification
  • Saving your game progress
  • Providing a personalized experience

We do not access your Google account password or any private Google account data.

3. Advertising

Teks Flip may display third-party advertisements, including banner ads and rewarded ads. These services (such as Google AdMob) may collect and use data to provide relevant ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

These third-party services may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your in-game account
  • To save your progress
  • To operate and improve the Game
  • To fix bugs and monitor performance
  • To serve advertisements
  • To prevent abuse and technical issues

5. Third-Party Services

The Game may rely on third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Sign-In

These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies for more details.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with third-party service providers only as necessary to operate the Game, serve ads, or comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Retention

Your account information (name and email) is retained while your account is active. Other data collected by third-party services may be retained according to their policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices

You may limit ad personalization through your device or Google account settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Teks Flip is not directed specifically to children under the age required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us via email.

Upon request, we will delete your account information (including name and email) within a reasonable timeframe, unless retention is required for legal reasons.
